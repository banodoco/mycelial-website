## STRUCTURE
Cause: Final retires on its own clock (retireScale/retireCost fitted to a "window"), not the camera's blend phase; the web/mote restore, shed fog, bed/sky and group.visible each re-derive the lap from that clock, so they are unmasked relative to what the camera/lens show. This fix moves only the web/mote/shed-fog restore onto `frame.transitionPhase` (first production reader). The phase is the camera's smootherstep so I invert it to time-linear (otherwise a window on e saturates early: 10-90% only 1.1-1.5 s for any start that leaves the colony clear). Cleaner design: publish `{t, dur}` on transitionPhase (O3 section 5) so no inversion is needed and migrate bed/sky/visible/retireEff too, deleting retireScale/retireCost/floorHold/floorPullOf for the wrap. Cost: touches journey/frame/publication.js (not my files) plus reworking 13 pinned patches/tests (tempo-run, epilogue-retire); medium-high risk to the ten goldens' in-flight paths (rests are unaffected).
