# Editing the deck

- **Copy:** The slide sections are in `index.html`, in order from slide 1 through slide 11. The current intro story for slides 4–7 and its scoped responsive typography are in the opening block of `deck-overlay.js`; edit that block for “The solution”, “Meet Astrid”, “Everyone’s to shape” and “How it pays”. It runs synchronously before scene registration, updates the existing semantic headings without replacing them, and suppresses their older fallback body copy. The complete intro remains inside each heading so the existing scene text bounds, masks and transitions include it. Other slides’ main narrative copy remains in `index.html`. Some visuals and labels are generated by the slide script named in the nearby HTML comments.
- **Intro checks:** Run `node --test tests/deck-story.test.mjs` for canonical copy, links, idempotence, paired marker timing, shared explanations, reset/exit cleanup, scroll visibility, reduced motion, overlay interaction and arrival pacing. These dependency-free tests use DOM and clock fixtures; they do not replace real-scene browser or visual checks. Keep scene lifetime and restoration contracts intact when adjusting the intro’s visual hierarchy.
- **Slide 5 header:** App and Vision are separate new-tab links under the title; the highlighted Astrid name is not a link. “Meet Astrid” stays on one line above App and Vision. Content-sized columns group the two roadmap lines, brace and “All powered by Open Models” closely; unused horizontal space stays on the right. Slide 5's numbered section label reads “Introducing.” The name glows once after the slide settles. On portrait phones, Today and Soon become equal, centered columns; the desktop brace is replaced by a horizontal, upward-branching connector above the centered “All powered by Open Models” caption. Reduced-motion preferences disable the glow.
- **Demo notes and pacing:** `createAstridDemoNotes` in `deck-overlay.js` creates two controls for each of three explanations: one attached to its chat step and one to its timeline output. A new chat step is visible for 500 ms before its marker pulses. The editor marker follows 850 ms after that reveal on the visit clock, never before the output exists. Both delays are cancelled on reset or exit. The image explanation stays at the centre of the reference-image row. Both controls share the same hover, focus and tap explanation and each pulses once per tour. Chat markers track scrolling and hide outside the chat viewport. Reset cancels delayed reveals and animations; reduced motion skips both animation and delay. `demo.js` retains its full-final-film playback and thirty-second final-frame hold. Reading an open note pauses the story countdown. `deckStoryPacing` retains 1.45× forward animated arrivals on slide 7 and 1.3× on slide 8; direct, reduced-motion, reverse and settled visits keep their native pace.
- **Figures and links:** Slides 10–11's initiative data and links are in `flywheel.js`. Slide 8's illustrative people counter and timing are in `flowers.js`.
- **Slide 1 subtitle and its note:** The subtitle is the `.body` under slide 1's headline in `index.html`; its copy is one line (the `&nbsp;` keeps the dash with its phrase). The gold ¹ after "economy" opens `.vc-note`, the card just after slide 1's `.split`: its two bars (in the inline SVG), its line and its source link are edited there. The figures are Goldman Sachs Research's (19 April 2023): $250 billion in 2023, $480 billion by 2027, and 50 million creators growing 10–20% a year.
- **Slide 1 recipients:** The seven `.vc-tile` entries are in the slide 1 markup in `index.html`; they stand round the ring in markup order (which is also the Tab order), and `collage.js` lays the ring out for however many there are. Each uses a `<picture>` with an AVIF source and JPEG fallback still, a `data-preview` MP4 that plays while the card is at the front, and `data-film` for its Cloudflare Stream overlay film. Every card is square: `data-square` is the object-position that crops its still and preview to the square, and `data-focus` places the still within its film's 16:9 frame, so the player opens out of the card. The tile's `data-title` and `data-artist` supply its title and artist (the name inside each card's top edge is generated from them); the `data-page` is its external entry link. The ring's label and its Arca Gidan link are in `.vc-label` in the same markup.
- **Link preview image:** `mycelial-og.jpg` is a 1200×630 JPEG captured from slide 1 at 2× and downsampled, with the footer chrome and motion ring hidden. Start the local server with `PORT=8240 python3 serve.py`, open `http://127.0.0.1:8240/mycelial/index.html` in Playwright at a 1200×630 viewport and device scale factor 2, wait for the opening to settle, inject CSS to hide `.chrome` and `#motion`, and save the screenshot. Downsample it to 1200×630, export JPEG under 300 KB, and inspect it before replacing the image.
