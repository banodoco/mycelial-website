# Editing the deck

- **Copy:** The slide sections are in `index.html`, in order from slide 1 through slide 11. The current intro story for slides 4–7 and its scoped responsive typography are in the opening block of `deck-overlay.js`; edit that block for “The solution”, “Meet Astrid”, “Yours to shape” and “How it pays”. It runs synchronously before scene registration, updates the existing semantic headings without replacing them, and suppresses their older fallback body copy. The complete intro remains inside each heading so the existing scene text bounds, masks and transitions include it. Other slides’ main narrative copy remains in `index.html`. Some visuals and labels are generated by the slide script named in the nearby HTML comments.
- **Intro checks:** Run `node --test tests/deck-story.test.mjs` for the synchronous copy, node identity, idempotence and overlay interaction checks. These are dependency-free unit checks, not a substitute for the real-scene browser checks below. Keep scene lifetime and restoration contracts intact when adjusting the intro’s visual hierarchy.
- **Demo notes and pacing:** `createAstridDemoNotes` in `deck-overlay.js` provides the three hover, focus and tap explanations attached to the source scene, second reference frame and final output. `demo.js` updates them at production beats, waits for the full final film, then holds its final frame for ten seconds. Reading an open note pauses the story countdown. `deckStoryPacing` integrates the shared clock at 1.45× for forward animated arrivals on slide 7 and 1.3× for slide 8; direct, reduced-motion, reverse and settled visits retain their native pace. The dependency-free story tests also cover pacing and terminal playback.
- **Figures and links:** Slides 10–11's initiative data and links are in `flywheel.js`. Slide 8's illustrative people counter and timing are in `flowers.js`.
- **Slide 1 subtitle and its note:** The subtitle is the `.body` under slide 1's headline in `index.html`; its copy is one line (the `&nbsp;` keeps the dash with its phrase). The gold ¹ after "economy" opens `.vc-note`, the card just after slide 1's `.split`: its two bars (in the inline SVG), its line and its source link are edited there. The figures are Goldman Sachs Research's (19 April 2023): $250 billion in 2023, $480 billion by 2027, and 50 million creators growing 10–20% a year.
- **Slide 1 recipients:** The seven `.vc-tile` entries are in the slide 1 markup in `index.html`; they stand round the ring in markup order (which is also the Tab order), and `collage.js` lays the ring out for however many there are. Each uses a `<picture>` with an AVIF source and JPEG fallback still, a `data-preview` MP4 that plays while the card is at the front, and `data-film` for its Cloudflare Stream overlay film. Every card is square: `data-square` is the object-position that crops its still and preview to the square, and `data-focus` places the still within its film's 16:9 frame, so the player opens out of the card. The tile's `data-title` and `data-artist` supply its title and artist (the name inside each card's top edge is generated from them); the `data-page` is its external entry link. The ring's label and its Arca Gidan link are in `.vc-label` in the same markup.
- **Link preview image:** `mycelial-og.jpg` is a 1200×630 JPEG captured from slide 1 at 2× and downsampled, with the footer chrome and motion ring hidden. Start the local server with `PORT=8240 python3 serve.py`, open `http://127.0.0.1:8240/mycelial/index.html` in Playwright at a 1200×630 viewport and device scale factor 2, wait for the opening to settle, inject CSS to hide `.chrome` and `#motion`, and save the screenshot. Downsample it to 1200×630, export JPEG under 300 KB, and inspect it before replacing the image.
- **Lifecycle and checks:** See [LIFECYCLE.md](LIFECYCLE.md) for scene ownership, restoration, focused commands and their environment limitations.
- **Publish:** Follow [PUBLISH.md](../tools/deck/PUBLISH.md) to build a site copy, stamp its asset versions and set the absolute preview image URL for the deployment base.
