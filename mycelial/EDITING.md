# Editing the deck

- **Copy:** The slide sections and their main narrative copy are in `index.html`, in order from slide 1 through slide 11. Some visuals and labels are generated by the slide script named in the nearby HTML comments.
- **Figures and links:** Slides 10–11's initiative data and links are in `flywheel.js`. Slide 8's illustrative people counter and timing are in `flowers.js`.
- **Slide 1 subtitle and its note:** The subtitle is the `.body` under slide 1's headline in `index.html`; its copy is one line (the `&nbsp;` keeps the dash with its phrase). The gold ¹ after "accelerating" opens `.vc-note`, the card just after slide 1's `.split`: its two bars (in the inline SVG), its line and its source link are edited there. The figures are Goldman Sachs Research's (19 April 2023): $250 billion in 2023, $480 billion by 2027, and 50 million creators growing 10–20% a year.
- **Slide 1 recipients:** The seven `.vc-tile` entries are in the slide 1 markup in `index.html`; they stand round the ring in markup order (which is also the Tab order), and `collage.js` lays the ring out for however many there are. Each uses a `<picture>` with an AVIF source and JPEG fallback still, a `data-preview` MP4 that plays while the card is at the front, and `data-film` for its Cloudflare Stream overlay film. Every card is 4:5: `data-crop` is the object-position that crops a still and preview of another shape to it, and `data-focus` places the still within its film's 16:9 frame, so the player opens out of the card. The tile's `data-title` and `data-artist` supply its title and artist (the name inside each card's top edge is generated from them); `data-page` is its external entry link. The ring's label and its Arca Gidan link are in `.vc-label` in the same markup.
- **Link preview image:** `mycelial-og.jpg` is a 1200×630 JPEG captured from slide 1 at 2× and downsampled, with the footer chrome and motion ring hidden. Start the local server with `PORT=8240 python3 serve.py`, open `http://127.0.0.1:8240/mycelial/index.html` in Playwright at a 1200×630 viewport and device scale factor 2, wait for the opening to settle, inject CSS to hide `.chrome` and `#motion`, and save the screenshot. Downsample it to 1200×630, export JPEG under 300 KB, and inspect it before replacing the image.
- **Checks:** Start the local server with `PORT=8240 python3 serve.py` and run `node tools/deck/check-stress.mjs http://127.0.0.1:8240/mycelial/index.html`. Run `node tools/deck/check-firstload.mjs http://127.0.0.1:8240/mycelial/index.html` to check the first load.
- **Publish:** Follow [PUBLISH.md](../tools/deck/PUBLISH.md) to build a site copy, stamp its asset versions and set the absolute preview image URL for the deployment base.
